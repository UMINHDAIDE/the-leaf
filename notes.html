Uniform continuity.

The difference between choosing a distance for each point and choosing one distance that works everywhere.

REAL ANALYSISREADING NOTE · 6 MIN16 NOVEMBER 2026

Continuity is a local condition: near each point, the function behaves in a controlled way. Uniform continuity asks for something stronger. The same input tolerance must work at every point in the domain.

1. The definition

A function \(f:A\to\mathbb R\) is uniformly continuous on \(A\) if

\[\forall\varepsilon>0,\ \exists\delta>0,\ \forall x,y\in A:\quad |x-y|<\delta\implies |f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.\]

Notice the order of the quantifiers. We choose \(\varepsilon\), then choose a single \(\delta\). Only after that do we consider all pairs \(x,y\in A\). The value of \(\delta\) cannot depend on the particular point.

2. Compare with ordinary continuity

Continuity at every point says that for each fixed \(a\in A\) and each \(\varepsilon>0\), there is a radius \(\delta>0\) such that

\[|x-a|<\delta\implies |f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon.\]

Here, the chosen \(\delta\) may depend on both \(a\) and \(\varepsilon\). Uniform continuity removes the dependence on the point.

Quantifier intuition. Continuity: for every point, a suitable neighbourhood exists. Uniform continuity: one common neighbourhood size works across the entire domain.

3. A standard example

Consider \(f(x)=x^2\) on \(\mathbb R\). It is continuous everywhere, but it is not uniformly continuous on \(\mathbb R\).

To see why, choose \(\varepsilon=1\). Given any \(\delta>0\), choose a positive integer \(n\) large enough that \(1/n<\delta\), and set \(x=n\), \(y=n+1/n\). Then

\[|x-y|=\frac1n<\delta,\qquad |x^2-y^2|=2+\frac1{n^2}>1.\]

Thus no matter how small the proposed \(\delta\) is, we can find two sufficiently close inputs whose outputs differ by more than 1.

4. The useful theorem

One important result is that every continuous function on a compact subset of \(\mathbb R\) is uniformly continuous. Compactness prevents the domain from stretching out indefinitely, and continuity can then be controlled with one global choice of \(\delta\).
