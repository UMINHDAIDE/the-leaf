SAMPLE MATHEMATICAL NOTE

Uniform continuity

A function \(f:A\to\mathbb{R}\) is uniformly continuous on \(A\) if for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists \(\delta>0\) such that for all \(x,y\in A\),

\[|x-y|<\delta\quad\Longrightarrow\quad |f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.\]

The key point is that \(\delta\) depends on \(\varepsilon\), but not on the particular choice of \(x\) and \(y\).
