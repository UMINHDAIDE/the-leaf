A SMALL INTRODUCTION

Why The Leaf?

A name shaped by humility, curiosity, and the belief that knowledge grows when we share it.

The Leaf is a personal mathematics blog where I share my mathematical explorations, personal perspectives, and learning journey.

A simple metaphor

A leaf, on its own, may seem small. It does not claim to be the whole tree, let alone the forest. Yet countless leaves together make something larger than any one of them.

I see a connection with mathematics. An idea may begin as a modest observation, a question in the margin, or an argument that is not yet complete. By itself, it may appear insignificant. Shared, examined, corrected, and developed alongside other ideas, it can become part of a much richer body of knowledge.

Learning with humility

Mathematics advances not only through individual insight, but also through the willingness to listen, question, and reconsider. Different perspectives can reveal assumptions we have overlooked and paths we might never have found alone.

The Leaf is my small reminder to approach mathematics with humility: to stay curious, to be open to correction, and to recognise the contributions of others. I hope this space can be a place where ideas are shared thoughtfully and where learning remains an ongoing process.

Small leaves, greater knowledge.
A small contribution is still a contribution. And together, small contributions can help something meaningful grow.

Thank you for visiting. I hope you find something here that makes you curious enough to ask another question.
