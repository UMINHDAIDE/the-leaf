THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

About The Leaf

A personal space for curiosity, humility, and mathematical exploration.

The Leaf is a personal mathematics blog where I share my mathematical explorations, personal perspectives, and learning journey.

From discovering new concepts and studying mathematical theories to exploring interesting problems and developing my own ideas, this is a place to document my growth, reflect on what I learn, and share my passion for mathematics with others.

Why The Leaf?

The Leaf is a nickname that reflects a quality I deeply admire in mathematicians: humility.

Mathematics is not built by individuals working in isolation. It grows through listening to others, embracing different perspectives, and sharing ideas. I believe that this sense of togetherness is among the most important foundations for developing, advancing, and discovering better mathematical languages.

A single leaf may seem small and insignificant, yet countless leaves sustain the vitality of our Earth. Perhaps, in its own quiet way, a leaf represents one of the most remarkable elements of our universe.

Mathematical ideas and discoveries may appear insignificant at first. Yet we should never underestimate their potential. Like a leaf, each of us should follow our own path, remain humble in the face of what we do not know, and persevere in the hope of contributing something meaningful to a better world.

This is what The Leaf represents to me: humility, curiosity, perseverance, and the belief that even the smallest contributions can become part of something greater.

Small leaves, greater knowledge.
