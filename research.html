Extremal structure and rigidity on the hypercube

Exploring extremal functionals on the Boolean hypercube, with particular interest in stability and structural rigidity: when a configuration is close to optimal, what can be said about its shape?

How I use this page

Research is rarely a straight line. This page is intended as a transparent record of questions and progress, not a list of claims to novelty. Any result shared here should include its assumptions, definitions, and proof status.
